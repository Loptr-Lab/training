# Training Curriculum

Welcome to the Loptr Lab training curriculum. This space contains structured learning tracks, milestone exercises, and onboarding materials for contributors.

## Overview

The curriculum is designed to bring new contributors up to speed with the tools, practices, and architecture used across Loptr Lab projects. It is divided into tracks that can be followed independently or in sequence depending on your background and goals.

- **Track A** — Foundational TypeScript programming practice through milestone-based exercises.
- **Track B** — Architecture, design systems, and production-facing engine development.

## Repository Distinction

This curriculum references two related but different repositories:

- **Engine project:** the real Veiled Dominion game/engine repository, used for architecture, design, systems, and production-facing development.
- **Candidate exercise repo:** a separate, self-contained TypeScript exercise used for training and evaluation.

Participants following Track A may use the exercise repo for milestone-based programming practice before contributing to the full engine project.
