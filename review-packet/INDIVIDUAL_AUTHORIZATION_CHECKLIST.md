# Individual Authorization Checklist

Complete privately with the participant and agency. Do not commit completed copies to GitHub.

- [ ] Participant is connected with the responsible agency
- [ ] Employment goal is documented
- [ ] Proposed Loptr Lab activity is described
- [ ] Service category is confirmed by the agency
- [ ] Authorized provider/purchasing route is confirmed
- [ ] Start and end dates are written
- [ ] Authorized hours are written
- [ ] Cost and payer are written
- [ ] Participant financial responsibility is written
- [ ] Compensation/wage status is written
- [ ] Accommodations and accessible formats are written
- [ ] Instructor/supervisor requirements are confirmed
- [ ] Attendance, progress, and outcome reports are defined
- [ ] Privacy and release requirements are defined
- [ ] Benefits-counseling referral is made when applicable
- [ ] Required reporting to benefits/housing/other programs is identified
- [ ] Written authorization is received before funded service begins
- [ ] Authorization expiration and change process are recorded

**Final status:** APPROVED / APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS / MORE INFORMATION REQUIRED / DIFFERENT PROVIDER REQUIRED / NOT APPROVED / UNRESOLVED
