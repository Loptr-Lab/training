# Loptr Lab Training — Program Summary for Agency Review

**Status:** Proposed curriculum; not represented as an approved provider or guaranteed funding source.

## Purpose

Loptr Lab provides an accessibility-first, project-based learning pathway in software, game systems, technical writing, technical art, documentation, and creative technology. Participants produce documented artifacts that can demonstrate skills relevant to an individualized employment goal.

## Delivery

- Proposed format: remote, project-based instruction and supervised practice
- Repository: https://github.com/Loptr-Lab/training
- Accessibility: individualized accommodation planning; accessible documents and alternate communication methods should be confirmed before service
- Private records: maintained outside public GitHub
- Employment: participation is not a job offer or placement guarantee
- Compensation: no wage, stipend, contractor payment, or unpaid-work expectation should be inferred; the agency and written participation terms must classify each activity before it begins

## Proposed outcomes

Depending on the approved plan, a participant may demonstrate:

- Git and GitHub workflow;
- TypeScript and test-driven development;
- systems and accessibility documentation;
- technical writing and production handoff;
- accessible creative-tool or game-asset development;
- portfolio packaging and communication of limitations, rights, and provenance.

## Information requiring agency confirmation

- relationship to the participant's employment goal;
- service category;
- approved provider or purchasing mechanism;
- dates, hours, cost, and payment authorization;
- instructor/supervisor qualification requirements;
- accommodations;
- progress and outcome reporting;
- wage classification for project, work-experience, or internship activity;
- privacy, retention, insurance, and audit requirements.

No service should be described as agency-funded until written authorization is received.
